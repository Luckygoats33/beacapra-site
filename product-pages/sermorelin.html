What is BPC-157?

BPC-157 (Body Protection Compound-157) is a synthetic pentadecapeptide composed of 15 amino acids, derived from a protective protein found in human gastric juice. First isolated and characterized in the early 1990s, BPC-157 has become one of the most extensively studied peptides in regenerative research, with over 100 published studies examining its mechanisms and effects across multiple biological systems.

The peptide's amino acid sequence — Gly-Glu-Pro-Pro-Pro-Gly-Lys-Pro-Ala-Asp-Asp-Ala-Gly-Leu-Val — is stable in gastric juice, which distinguishes it from many other bioactive peptides that degrade rapidly in acidic environments. This stability is one of the key properties that has attracted significant research interest.

Mechanism of Action

Research suggests that BPC-157 operates through multiple interconnected pathways. It has been shown to interact with the nitric oxide (NO) system, promote angiogenesis (new blood vessel formation), and modulate growth factor expression including VEGF, EGF, and FGF. Studies have also demonstrated its involvement in the FAK-paxillin pathway, which plays a critical role in cell migration and tissue repair processes.

Additional research indicates that BPC-157 may influence the GABAergic and dopaminergic systems, suggesting potential applications beyond tissue repair. Its cytoprotective properties have been documented across gastrointestinal, musculoskeletal, and neurological research models.

Storage Instructions

  • Lyophilized (powder): Store at -20°C for long-term stability. Stable at room temperature during shipping.
  • Reconstituted: Store at 2-8°C (refrigerator). Use within 30 days of reconstitution.
  • Protect from light and repeated freeze-thaw cycles.
  • Reconstitute with bacteriostatic water (BAC water) for multi-use applications.

Reconstitution

Add 2mL of bacteriostatic water to the 5mg vial for a concentration of 2.5mg/mL (2,500mcg/mL). Direct the stream of water along the inside wall of the vial; do not inject directly onto the lyophilized powder. Gently swirl — do not shake — until fully dissolved. The solution should be clear and colorless.
