What is the BPC-157 + TB-500 Blend?

The Foundation Stack combines two of the most extensively researched healing peptides into a single lyophilized vial: BPC-157 (5mg) and TB-500 (5mg). This combination leverages the complementary mechanisms of both peptides — BPC-157's nitric oxide modulation and growth factor upregulation paired with TB-500's actin-sequestering and cell-migration properties — to provide researchers with a comprehensive tool for tissue repair studies.

Published research suggests that these two peptides operate through distinct but synergistic pathways. While BPC-157 primarily modulates the NO system and promotes angiogenesis via VEGF upregulation, TB-500 acts through actin polymerization regulation and integrin-linked kinase activation. The combination has become one of the most requested research protocols in the regenerative medicine field.

Mechanism of Action

The blend provides multi-pathway coverage: BPC-157 promotes new blood vessel formation and modulates inflammatory responses through the FAK-paxillin pathway, while TB-500 facilitates cell migration and extracellular matrix deposition through G-actin sequestration. Together, they address both the vascular and cellular components of the tissue repair cascade.

Storage Instructions

  • Lyophilized: Store at -20°C. Stable during shipping at ambient temperature.
  • Reconstituted: Store at 2-8°C. Use within 21 days.
  • Protect from light and avoid repeated freeze-thaw cycles.

Reconstitution

Add 2mL of bacteriostatic water for a combined concentration of 5mg/mL (2.5mg/mL each peptide). Swirl gently until dissolved.
